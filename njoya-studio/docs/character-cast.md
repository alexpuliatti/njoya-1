# Character studies

The approved builder animation is on main. New studies stay on the local motion branch until reviewed.

## 01 — The builder

Stone head, green reaching arm, purple eyes. Deliberate and a little stubborn. Pulls the W into place, then stands on BUILD. The approved size is 20% larger than the first miniature. Preview: `/motion`.

## 02 — The explorer

Pink curved head, woven shoulder, green fringe, brown limbs. Curious and nimble. First movement: rolls the O into WORLDS, steadies it and steps aside. Preview: `/motion/explorer`. This study is local only.

Asset: `public/media/motion/explorer.webp`, a transparent 768 × 615 WebP prepared from the user's pink-and-green character reference with built-in image_gen. The exact generation and cleanup prompts are saved in `docs/explorer-prompts.json`. No 3D model is used.

## 03 — The observer

Red face, blue eyelids, patterned legs and a small stone mask. A quiet companion: peeks from behind a project, blinks once, takes another look and retreats. Preview: `/motion/observer`. The project image remains a working link to the Brides de Galaxy Lab page.

Assets: `public/media/motion/observer.webp` and `observer-blink.webp`, two transparent 768 × 768 WebPs prepared with built-in image_gen from the user's red character reference. Only the closed-eye regions overlay the original character during the blink, keeping its body and textures steady. Exact prompts and eye coordinates are saved in `docs/observer-prompts.json`.

## Homepage cast

The local homepage card shares one semantic headline across all three characters. Once the artwork is ready and the existing intro clears, they start together: the builder pulls the W, the explorer rolls the O into the same WORLDS line, and the observer peeks around the card edge. The approved builder size and standing position remain. The scene plays once, leaves the card link available, pauses in a hidden tab, and presents a readable still headline for reduced motion or artwork errors. These additions remain on the local motion branch; main contains the previously approved builder only.

The cast should feel like it inhabits the typography and edges of the work. Each moment has a short action and a clear finish; larger appearances belong in the Lab, with occasional smaller appearances elsewhere.
